Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about EventPulse registration and tickets.

How do I receive my event ticket pass?

Once your registration is submitted, a verified digital boarding pass with a simulated QR check-in code and booking reference number is instantly generated and stored securely in your browser's LocalStorage. You can also print or export it as a PDF at any time.

Can I cancel my registration if my plans change?

Yes! Click the "My Tickets" button in the navigation header to open your registration hub. You can view passes or cancel any registration with a single click.

Are student discounts available?

Yes, select events offer dedicated Student & Early Bird passes at heavily discounted rates. Just select the Student Pass tier when registering.

Does EventPulse support dark mode?

Yes! EventPulse features complete dark and light themes that remember your preference across sessions using LocalStorage, with smooth contrast-compliant styling.
